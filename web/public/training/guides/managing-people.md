# Managing people

## The directory

**People** lists everyone in the workspace. Columns: avatar, name and title (or email), company, circles, sources, strength with interaction count, and last contact date.

- **Search** matches name, company, title, emails and notes.
- **Source**, **Company** and **Tag** drop-downs narrow the list.
- **Circle chips** combine (both Personal *and* Business). Turn on **Only these circles (exact)** to see people in exactly that combination — the same lists the Venn and chord views link to.
- **Sort** by strength, recency, interactions, name or company.
- **Export CSV** downloads the current filtered list.

## Profiles

Open any row to see the full profile:

- **Header** — name, title, company, with **In graph** to jump to the network centred on them.
- **Circles** — tap chips to override the inferred circles; **Reset to automatic** returns control to the importer.
- **Contact details** — emails, phones and location with mail/tel links.
- **Sources** — each import that contributed, with a link to the original profile.
- **Tags & notes** — free-form tags (press Enter) and private notes.
- **Activity** — the last 24 months plus the list of interactions. **Log interaction** adds meetings, calls or messages by hand; they count towards strength.
- **Connected people** — everyone linked to this person and why (same employer, email thread, or a link you added).

The **⋮ menu** has Edit details, Log an interaction, Merge with…, and Delete.

## Adding people manually

**Add person** on the People page creates someone with a name, email, company, title and circles. Manual people take part in matching like everyone else, so a later import will merge into them.

## Duplicates

Imports match by external ID, email, handle and exact name. For the rest:

- **Find duplicates** (merge icon on the People page) lists candidate pairs with the reason (same email, same name). **Keep this** merges the other record into the one you chose.
- **Merge with…** on a profile lets you search for the duplicate yourself.

Merging combines emails, phones, tags, sources and interactions, keeps the chosen name and recomputes circles and strength.

## Roles

Viewers can browse everything but cannot edit, import or change settings. Editors can do all of that. Only the owner manages members and can delete the workspace.
