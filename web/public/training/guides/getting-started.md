# Getting started

Relationship Manager gathers every connection you have — LinkedIn, Facebook, X, your address book and your email — into one network you can explore from four angles: personal, business, corporate and social.

## Create an account

1. Open the app and choose **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters). Your email is used as one of "your addresses" for email imports.
3. You land on your dashboard with a workspace called **My network**.

If you were sent an invite link (`/invite/…`), open it first: signing up through the link puts you straight into the shared workspace.

## The four circles

| Circle | Who | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | Friends, family, people you know outside work | Facebook friends, address book, personal (free-mail) email addresses |
| **Business** | Professional contacts outside your company | LinkedIn connections, work email with other organisations |
| **Corporate** | Colleagues inside your organisation | Email addresses on your corporate domains, or a matching company name |
| **Social** | The wider public network | X followers and follows, Facebook followers |

A person can be in several circles at once. Circles are inferred automatically from sources and email domains; you can override them on any profile.

## The dashboard

- **Stat cards** — people, relationships, interactions and companies. Click one to jump to the matching view.
- **Circle cards** — counts per circle; click to open the network graph with that lens.
- **Activity** — interactions per month over the last 24 months.
- **Strongest relationships** — people ranked by the strength score.
- **Recent imports** — a log of what was imported and what it produced.

## Relationship strength

Every person has a strength from 0 to 100 %. It combines:

- how many sources know the person (LinkedIn *and* Facebook *and* email counts more than one),
- how many interactions you've had (email threads and anything you log),
- whether the conversation is two-way,
- how recent the last interaction was.

Strength drives node size in the graph, box size in the treemap and the default sort order in People.

## Navigation

The rail on the left (bottom bar on phones) has **Home**, **Network**, **People**, **Import**, **Learn** and **Settings**. The workspace switcher is at the top of the rail; your account menu (install app, sign out) is at the bottom.
